## 1. โจทย์จริงจาก iJudge

<br />There are two gift boxes for you. One from your friend, and one from me. <br>
For what any reason, you know the weight of the gift box from me (<em>k</em>), and the average weight of these two gift boxes (<em>m</em>).<br>
Please find the weight of the gift box from your friend.

## 2. Input Specification

**<u>2 lines. </u>**
The first line is the average weight of two gift boxes (<em>m</em>) in kilograms. The second line is the weight of the gift box (<em>k</em>) in kilograms from me. <u>(both are floating number)</u> <br />

**1st line:** <em>boxes (m)</em><br />
**2nd line:** <em> box (k)</em>

## 3. Output Specification

1 line which is the weight of a gift box from your friend in kilograms. The result is shown as a <u>**floating number**</u>.

<u>**1st line:**</u> `the weight of a gift box from your friend in kilograms`
